Readings

Thirty articles on what the Orthodox Church believes and why — written for anyone curious, and especially for Christians from other traditions who want a clear account rather than an argument.

These pages set out the Orthodox position on its own terms. They are not written against anyone. Where Orthodox Christians differ from other Christians, the difference is stated plainly and the reasoning given, so that a reader can weigh it. Many of them draw on the writings of Fr. Seraphim Rose (1934–1982), who came to Orthodoxy from a Protestant background and who insisted that harshness towards other Christians is, in his own published words, “spiritually wrong” even when it is technically correct.
