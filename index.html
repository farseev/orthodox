    Questions

    What teams ask first.

    How is this different from a social listening tool?
    Social listening tells you what's being said. SOMIN tells you what's working -- every competitor ad parsed for proposition, persona, emotion, and predicted performance, then connected to the audience tensions driving the engagement. The output is concepts ready for review, not a sentiment dashboard.
    Is SOMIN only for brand marketers, or does it work for agencies and creators too?
    All three, on the same category engine. Brand teams use it to defend campaigns with evidence (brand playbook), agencies use it to pitch faster with a data-backed edge (agency playbook), and creator programs use it to territory-map a roster and auto-brief affiliates in their own voice (creator playbook). See the "Four playbooks" section above, or jump straight to the page for your seat.
    What's the difference between using the SOMIN platform and using the AI agents?
    The platform (Brand Tracker, Perspective Studies, Expressions, Ad Scoring) is the workspace a strategist or planner drives by hand. The six AI agents run those same jobs autonomously end to end -- strategy, paid media, influencer & GMV, social posting, CRM & email, and reporting -- with a human sign-off gate at every step and a kill switch that activates in under 60 seconds. Same category signal underneath either way.
    Is the 13-minute claim real, or marketing math?
    It's measured -- and independently documented in Google Cloud's customer story on SOMIN. Median time from brief upload to a stack of pre-scored creative concepts across our active customer base is 13 minutes, with campaign planning and creative analysis running up to 12× faster than manual workflows. The Mothercare, KPI Media, and Click2View case studies all document the before-and-after numbers in detail.
    Where does the data come from?
    Public ad inventories from Meta, Google, TikTok, and YouTube. Engagement signal from live in-feed surfaces. First-party audience data from licensed partners. We do not scrape gated content and we do not buy data we can't audit. Data handling is aligned with the GDPR and Singapore's PDPA, on Google Cloud infrastructure. The Singapore ride-hailing example above is built the same way -- public ad-library data, decoded automatically, no client data.
    How does SOMIN track competitor ad performance?
    SOMIN's Brand Tracker continuously pulls every public ad and organic post competitors run across Meta, Google, TikTok, and YouTube, then uses large language models to decode each creative into a structured object: proposition, persona, tension, emotion, format, and call-to-action. Each ad is scored for predicted engagement and mapped into a live category view -- share of voice, share of engagement, and white space -- refreshed daily across 11+ markets.
    How does AI reduce campaign research and pitch preparation time?
    SOMIN automates the manual parts of research -- collecting competitor creative, tagging it, clustering audiences, and drafting briefs -- in one workflow. Google Cloud's published case study documents idea generation dropping from days to minutes and planning cycles running up to 12× faster. In practice: KPI Media cut pitch cycles from one week to two days, Mothercare reclaimed 25+ hours of research per week, and Click2View compressed category analysis from 18 hours to 3.
    What AI models and infrastructure power SOMIN?
    SOMIN runs on Google Cloud. Gemini 2.5 Pro handles large-scale ad and video understanding, audience clustering, and insight generation, alongside GPT-4-class language models for content workflows. Creative performance is predicted by SOMIN's in-house deep-learning CTR models -- published at ACM WSDM 2023 as SoCraft -- hosted on Compute Engine with Cloud GPUs and reaching 78% click-through-rate prediction accuracy, about 3× the human baseline. An explainable-AI layer keeps every recommendation traceable to the underlying data.
    Is SOMIN's AI research peer-reviewed?
    Yes. The platform and its models are published at top-tier ACM venues: SOMIN: An Explainable AI and LLM Platform for Real-Time, Data-Driven Digital Marketing Strategy (ACM Multimedia 2025), MindFuse: Towards GenAI Explainability in Marketing Strategy Co-Creation (ACM Multimedia 2025), Will AI Make Agencies Obsolete? (ACM Multimedia 2025), Against Opacity: Explainable AI and Large Language Models for Effective Digital Advertising (ACM Multimedia 2023), and SoCraft: Advertiser-level Predictive Scoring for Creative Performance on Meta (ACM WSDM 2023). The research line goes back to SoMin.ai's influencer-discovery marketplace (ACM Multimedia 2018) and personality-driven content generation (ACM WSDM 2021).
